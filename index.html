01ABOUT

A SPACE FOR CREATIVE MINDS

Different minds.
Shared energy.

Creative Bungalow is a community-driven platform created for individuals with a creative mindset—designers, entrepreneurs, marketers, innovators, and professionals who believe in the power of ideas to shape businesses and industries.

The initiative explores how creativity, branding, design, marketing, technology, and innovation can help businesses adapt, grow, and stay relevant. Through insightful sessions, industry updates, real-world case studies, and collaborative discussions, participants gain exposure to emerging trends, creative strategies, AI-driven solutions, and developments influencing modern businesses.

More than an event, Creative Bungalow is building a thriving community of curious thinkers and creative problem-solvers who learn, share, collaborate, and grow together. It is a space where knowledge meets creativity, connections spark opportunities, and fresh perspectives inspire meaningful impact.

01 THE BEGINNING

First Edition

The inaugural edition of Creative Bungalow was held on February 22–23, 2025, in Kalpetta, Wayanad, bringing together 45 creative professionals from across Kerala, from Kasaragod to Thiruvananthapuram. Designed as an immersive experience for visualizers, graphic designers, creative thinkers, and aspiring innovators, the event created a platform for learning, collaboration, and meaningful connections.

Over two inspiring days, participants engaged in expert-led sessions on Artificial Intelligence in advertising and marketing, branding strategies, and the evolving role of creativity in business. Industry leaders including Ansar Note AI and Favor Francis shared insights, practical knowledge, and real-world experiences that helped attendees understand emerging trends and opportunities within the creative industry.

Beyond the sessions, Creative Bungalow fostered a strong sense of community through networking activities, feedback sessions, collaborative discussions, trekking, and campfire gatherings. These experiences encouraged participants to exchange ideas, build relationships, and learn from one another in a relaxed and inspiring environment.

The first edition marked the beginning of a growing creative community—one that connects passionate minds, promotes continuous learning, and empowers individuals to stay ahead in an ever-changing creative landscape.

02 THE COMMUNITY GROWS

Second Edition

Building on the success of its inaugural gathering, the Second Edition of Creative Bungalow was held on January 17–18, 2026, at Ward 80, Wayanad, bringing together 65 creative professionals, designers, entrepreneurs, marketers, and innovators from across Kerala. The event continued its mission of fostering a vibrant creative community through knowledge sharing, collaboration, and meaningful industry connections.

The two-day experience featured insightful sessions from some of the industry's leading professionals. Mr. Shaheer Brandler, Brand Identity Strategist, delivered an engaging session titled “From Scratch to Visual Identity,” offering participants a deep understanding of the strategic and creative process behind building memorable brands. Mr. Jaison Antony from Seasaw Kochi, one of the leading experts in production projects, shared valuable insights through his session “Production Secrets,” unveiling the realities, challenges, and best practices behind successful production workflows.

The event also hosted an inspiring Expert Talk Session featuring industry professionals including Mr. Faisal Hassainar Rawther, Mr. Rafeek KMC, Mr. Shinab Spase, and other experienced creative leaders who shared their journeys, industry perspectives, and practical lessons with the participants.

Beyond the sessions, Creative Bungalow continued to strengthen its growing community through networking, collaborative discussions, knowledge exchange, and interactive activities. The second edition reinforced the platform's vision of creating a space where creative minds can connect, learn from one another, and stay updated with the latest developments in branding, design, marketing, production, technology, and business innovation.
