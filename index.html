THE CREATIVE COMMUNITY

About Creative Bungalow

Creative Bungalow brings together designers, entrepreneurs, marketers and curious thinkers to explore creativity, branding, design, technology and innovation. Through expert sessions, shared ideas and collaboration, we build meaningful connections and inspire new possibilities.

01 THE BEGINNING

First Edition

Held on February 22–23, 2025, in Kalpetta, Wayanad, the first edition brought together 45 creative professionals from across Kerala for two days of learning and connection.

Sessions on AI in advertising, marketing and branding—led by Ansar Note AI and Favor Francis—were complemented by networking, discussions, trekking and a campfire.

02 THE COMMUNITY GROWS

Second Edition

Held on January 17–18, 2026, at Ward 80, Wayanad, the second edition welcomed 65 creative professionals from across Kerala.

Shaheer Brandler explored brand identity, while Jaison Antony shared production insights. An expert talk with Faisal Hassainar Rawther, Rafeek KMC, Shinab Spase and other creative leaders brought fresh perspectives, followed by networking and collaborative activities.
